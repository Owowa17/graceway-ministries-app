Install in four steps

  1. Tap Download the app. Wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the file. Tap the download notification, or find it in your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install. If Android says the file is blocked, tap Settings and allow installs from your browser, then go back and tap Install.

    If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The app is new and not on the Play Store yet.

  4. Open Graceway. You can switch the install permission off again in Settings.

Already have an older version? Uninstall it first, then install the new one. This page is for Android only. iPhone is not supported yet.

What is inside

Daily Grace
A new scripture, message and prayer every day, with one tap to share it on WhatsApp.
Prayer requests
Send a request to our prayer team and choose to keep it private.
Grace Wall
Read testimonies of what God has done, and share your own.
Need help
Ask for help with food, rent, school fees or counselling, and find the emergency and pastoral care contacts.
Giving
Our bank details, with a button to copy the account number.

In an emergency, call 112. The app is not watched around the clock, so use the phone for anything urgent. The 112 line may not be active in every state yet.

Your privacy

The app needs no account and has no ads. It only keeps what you choose to type:

  • Prayer requests, with an optional name and a private setting.
  • Help requests, with a phone or WhatsApp number so we can reach you.
  • Testimonies, which appear on the Grace Wall only after we review them.

This information is stored in the ministry's Google Sheet and is seen only by our pastoral care team. Please do not type bank details or ID numbers. To ask us to remove something you sent, use the Need to talk or pray? button in the app.
